✏️ Back Drawing Challenge

See it. Draw it. Pass it. Guess it!
How to play
  1. Students stand in a line, facing away from the screen.
  2. The student closest to the screen gets to see the symbol.
  3. The symbol remains visible only for the selected time.
  4. That student draws the symbol on the back of the next student.
  5. The drawing is passed from student to student.
  6. The final student says or writes what the symbol represents.
  7. The teacher reveals the answer and awards the point.
